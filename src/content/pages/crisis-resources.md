If you're in immediate danger, call **911** or go to your nearest
emergency room.

- **988** — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text 988 any hour;
  veterans press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line.
- **1-800-799-7233** — the National Domestic Violence Hotline. 24/7 phone,
  chat, and text support.
- **1-800-656-4673** — the RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline. 24/7
  phone and chat support for survivors of sexual violence.

This practice isn't a crisis service and isn't staffed around the clock —
nothing here is watched in an emergency, so please don't wait for a reply
to reach one of the lines above. Once you're stable and looking for
longer-term work, [start with a free consultation](/#contact).
